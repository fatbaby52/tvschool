Affiliate Disclosure

TV School is committed to transparency. This page explains how we earn money and how it affects (and doesn't affect) our recommendations.

How We Earn Money

TV School participates in affiliate advertising programs designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to retail partners. This means when you click on a link to a retailer on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

We are a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for us to earn fees by linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

We may also earn commissions from other retailers including but not limited to Best Buy, Walmart, Costco, and other TV retailers.

Editorial Independence

Our affiliate relationships do not influence our TV grades, recommendations, or value calculations. Our value prediction model is based purely on objective factors: screen size, panel technology, professional review scores, and brand positioning.

We grade TVs the same way regardless of which retailer offers the best price or whether we have an affiliate relationship with that retailer. A TV priced at Amazon gets the same grade calculation as one at Best Buy or any other store.

We will never:

  • Recommend a product because of affiliate commissions
  • Hide better deals from non-affiliate retailers
  • Inflate grades for products from affiliate partners
  • Accept payment to feature or promote specific products

Price Accuracy

We make every effort to display accurate and up-to-date pricing information. However, prices change frequently and may vary between the time we update our data and when you visit a retailer's site. Always verify the final price on the retailer's website before making a purchase.

Product prices and availability are accurate as of the date/time indicated on each product listing and are subject to change. Any price and availability information displayed on retailer websites at the time of purchase will apply to the purchase of that product.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or how we make recommendations, please don't hesitate to reach out. We believe in complete transparency about how TV School operates.
